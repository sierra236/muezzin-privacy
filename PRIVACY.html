Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Al-Muezzin mobile application (the “Application”), developed and provided by DOĞAN YİĞİT ŞAHİN (the “Service Provider”, “we”, “us”, or “our”). The Application is provided as a freemium service and is intended to help users view prayer times, Qibla direction, prayer reminders, prayer tracking, widgets, and related features.

Al-Muezzin is not an official application of Diyanet İşleri Başkanlığı or any other religious authority. Prayer times may be calculated using third-party calculation services and location-based methods. They may differ from local official announcements.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the Application, we may collect or process the following categories of information:

  • Account information: If you create an account or sign in, we may process your email address, display name, authentication provider information, and user identifier.
  • Guest usage information: You may use some features without registering. In guest mode, settings such as selected location, language, notification preferences, and prayer time cache may be stored locally on your device.
  • Location information: With your permission, we may use your precise or approximate location, including latitude, longitude, city, country, country code, and timezone, to calculate prayer times, determine Qibla direction, select the appropriate calculation method, and provide location-based reminders.
  • Manual location information: If you choose a city or district manually, we may store that selected location and related metadata.
  • Prayer tracking information: If you use tracking features, we may store prayer completion status, completion timestamps, goals, streaks, monthly progress, and related settings.
  • Notification information: If you enable notifications, we may process notification settings, selected prayers, reminder times, and push notification tokens.
  • Profile photo: If you upload a profile photo, it may be stored using cloud storage and linked to your user profile.
  • Device, usage, and diagnostic information: We may process device type, operating system, app version, crash logs, diagnostics, performance data, and general usage information to maintain and improve the Application.
  • Advertising information: If ads are shown, advertising partners may process identifiers, device information, ad interactions, and consent signals according to your platform settings and applicable law.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • calculate prayer times based on your location and selected or automatically resolved calculation method;
  • show Qibla direction using your location and device sensors;
  • send optional prayer reminders, prayer check reminders, daily content notifications, and widget or live countdown updates;
  • support prayer tracking, goals, streaks, and progress features;
  • sync your settings and profile across devices if you create an account;
  • provide guest mode features using local device storage;
  • display ads if advertising is enabled in the Application;
  • analyze crashes, fix bugs, improve performance, and secure the Application;
  • respond to support requests and comply with legal obligations.

3. Location Data and Prayer Time Services

Location is central to the Application’s prayer time and Qibla features. You can either allow location access or select a location manually. If you deny location permission, some features may be limited, but the Application should still allow manual location selection where available.

When prayer times are requested, your latitude, longitude, timezone, and country code may be sent to third-party prayer time calculation services, such as AlAdhan, to calculate prayer times. We do not send your name, email, profile photo, or prayer tracking history to AlAdhan for this purpose.

Qibla direction depends on your location, device compass, device sensors, and calibration quality. Sensor errors, magnetic interference, or inaccurate location may affect the displayed direction.

4. Notifications, Widgets, and Live Countdown Features

Notifications are optional. You can enable or disable prayer reminders, prayer check reminders, and daily content notifications in the Application settings or through your device settings.

If you enable widgets or live countdown features, the Application may write a small prayer countdown data payload to platform shared storage so that the widget or live activity can show the next prayer name, target time, countdown, progress, and location label. Widgets and live activities are subject to operating system limitations and may not update continuously on all devices.

5. Quiet Mode / Focus Mode

The Application may offer platform-specific features to help reduce interruptions during prayer times.

  • Android: If supported and if you explicitly grant the required permission, the Application may help enable Do Not Disturb during selected prayer periods and restore the previous state afterward.
  • iOS: iOS does not allow third-party apps to programmatically enable or disable Silent Mode, Do Not Disturb, or Focus. On iOS, the Application may provide a Prayer Focus setup guide and optional reminders, but it cannot automatically control Focus or Do Not Disturb.

6. Third-Party Services

The Application may use third-party services that process data according to their own privacy policies. Depending on the platform and enabled features, these may include:

  • Google Play Services
  • Firebase, including Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Storage, Cloud Messaging, and Analytics where enabled
  • Google AdMob
  • Expo
  • Sentry
  • AlAdhan
  • Apple services, if you use iOS platform features such as Sign in with Apple, notifications, widgets, or Live Activities

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers only as needed to operate, secure, analyze, advertise, or improve the Application, or as required by law.

7. Advertising and Tracking

The Application may display ads through Google AdMob or similar advertising services. Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your consent, platform settings, region, and applicable law.

On iOS, if tracking is used for personalized advertising or cross-app tracking, the Application will request permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework where required. You may manage tracking and ad personalization choices through your device settings and any consent controls shown in the Application.

8. Artificial Intelligence

The Application does not currently use artificial intelligence technologies to process your personal data or to generate prayer times. If AI-powered features are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain what data is used and how it is processed.

9. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain personal information for as long as needed to provide the Application, maintain your account, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our terms. Guest data stored locally on your device may be removed by clearing app data or uninstalling the Application.

Registered users can request account deletion from within the Application, where available, under Profile > Account > Delete Account. Deleting your account is intended to remove or anonymize account profile data and associated prayer tracking data, subject to legal, security, fraud-prevention, backup, or technical retention requirements.

You may also contact us at al-muezzin@al-muezzin.com for privacy or deletion requests.

10. Your Choices

You can control your information in the following ways:

  • disable location permission in your device settings;
  • choose a manual location instead of using GPS where supported;
  • enable or disable notifications in the Application or device settings;
  • manage advertising and tracking choices through platform settings and consent prompts;
  • delete your account from within the Application if you registered;
  • uninstall the Application to stop local data collection on your device.
