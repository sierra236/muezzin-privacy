Delete Account

Al-Muezzin account and data deletion request

This page explains how users of the Al-Muezzin mobile application can request deletion of their account and associated app data. Al-Muezzin is developed and provided by DOĞAN YİĞİT ŞAHİN.

For account deletion support, contact us at al-muezzin@al-muezzin.com.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Open the Al-Muezzin app.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Open Account.
  4. Select Delete Account, if this option is available in your app version.

If you cannot access the app or the in-app deletion option is not available, email us at al-muezzin@al-muezzin.com from the email address linked to your Al-Muezzin account. Please include the subject line Account Deletion Request.

Data Deleted or Anonymized

When your account deletion request is completed, we will delete or anonymize account-related data such as:

  • account profile information, including email address, display name, and authentication identifier;
  • profile photo, if uploaded;
  • cloud-synced prayer tracking information, goals, streaks, and progress data;
  • cloud-synced app settings linked to your account, where technically possible;
  • push notification tokens linked to your account.

Data That May Be Retained

Some information may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, payment, tax, accounting, backup, or technical reasons. This may include:

  • purchase and entitlement records handled through Apple, Google Play, and RevenueCat;
  • transaction, refund, and tax records required by app stores or payment providers;
  • security, crash, diagnostic, and abuse-prevention logs;
  • backup copies that are removed according to normal backup rotation schedules.

Locally stored guest data on your device can be removed by clearing app data or uninstalling the app. Deleting your Al-Muezzin account does not automatically cancel active subscriptions or refund purchases; those actions must be managed through Apple App Store or Google Play where applicable.

Processing Time

We aim to process account deletion requests within 30 days, unless a longer period is required by law, security needs, fraud-prevention obligations, unresolved disputes, or technical backup retention.

Contact

For questions about account deletion or privacy, contact al-muezzin@al-muezzin.com.
