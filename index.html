Al-Muezzin

Prayer times, Qibla direction, reminders, widgets, and related Islamic utility features.

Al-Muezzin is a mobile application designed to help users view prayer times, find the Qibla direction, schedule prayer reminders, use widgets, follow daily religious content, and manage related app settings. The app may offer an optional one-time purchase to remove ads. Core prayer features remain available without requiring a paid subscription.

Al-Muezzin is not an official application of Diyanet İşleri Başkanlığı or any other religious authority. Prayer times may be calculated using third-party services and location-based methods, and may differ from local official announcements.
